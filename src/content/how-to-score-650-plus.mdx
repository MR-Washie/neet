# The 650+ Score Blueprint

Achieving 650+ is not about studying 15 hours a day; it's about **intelligent consistency**.

## The 3 Pillars of Success
1. **NCERT is God:** 90% of Biology and 80% of Chemistry questions come directly from NCERT.
2. **The 3-Revision Rule:** Complete the syllabus 3 times before the final exam.
3. **Active Recall:** Stop re-reading notes. Start testing yourself with PYQs.
