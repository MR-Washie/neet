# Physics: From Fear to Favor

Physics often decides the rank. Most students fail here because they memorize formulas instead of understanding concepts.

## Steps to Success
1. **Understand the derivation:** It helps you understand the concept.
2. **Formula Sheet:** Create a sheet for every chapter.
3. **The 1-Hour Rule:** Practice at least 20 numericals daily, without looking at the solutions.
