# Revision is the Key

You will forget everything you read if you don't revise. Follow this schedule:
- **Daily:** Review the day's notes before sleeping.
- **Weekly:** Spend Sunday solving a full-length mock test.
- **Monthly:** Revise all chapters completed in that month.
