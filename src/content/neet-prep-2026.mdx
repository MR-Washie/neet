# Master Your NEET 2026 Journey

To succeed in NEET 2026, you need more than just hard work; you need **strategy**.

## Key Focus Areas
*   **NCERT Mastery:** 90% of Biology questions come directly from NCERT.
*   **Physics Practice:** Focus on mechanics and electrostatics.
*   **Consistent PYQs:** Solve at least one Previous Year Question paper every week.

Remember, consistency beats intensity every time!
