# Master Biology: The 360/360 Strategy

Biology is the backbone of your NEET score. Here is how you can ensure a perfect 360.

## Smart Techniques
* **Diagrams:** Don't just look at them; label them from memory.
* **Tables & Summaries:** Memorize all summary tables in NCERT.
* **Assertion-Reasoning:** These are the new trend. Practice at least 20 daily.
